THE SCIENCE

Why the populations change

01 · Plant growth

Plants grow according to available sunlight and rainfall, with temperature moving conditions away from an optimum.

02 · Herbivores

Herbivores increase when plant food is abundant and decrease when food becomes scarce.

03 · Predators

Predators depend on herbivores for food, creating a feedback loop between the two populations.

04 · Stability

The model compares population swings and survival to produce an educational stability score.
